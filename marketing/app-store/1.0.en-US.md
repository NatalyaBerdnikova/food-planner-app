## Description (≤4000)

```
You save recipes from Instagram, then lose them in the feed. Dinner becomes a last-minute decision. The grocery run is a second guess.

Food Planner keeps the full loop in one place: import a recipe, plan the week, buy what you need, and see how the day hits your calorie and macro targets.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Import from Instagram
Share a Reel or post into Food Planner. We pull ingredients, steps, and nutrition into an editable recipe card you can save to your library.

• Plan the week
Fill breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks for seven days. Flip between weeks when you want to plan ahead.

• Shop from the plan
Your grocery list updates from the meals you scheduled. Same items combine, categories stay grouped, and you can check off what you bought.

• Track nutrition that matches the plate
Daily calories, protein, fat, and carbs come from the ingredients in your plan. Set personal targets in onboarding, then focus on up to five micronutrients that matter to you.

• Build a recipe library
Browse ready-made recipes, create your own with photos and steps, or keep imports next to favorites in one library.

HOW IT FITS A BUSY WEEK

1. Share a recipe from Instagram or paste a link.
2. Review the draft and save it.
3. Place it on a day and meal slot.
4. Open Shopping and buy from the list.
5. Check Today to see progress toward your targets.

BUILT FOR PEOPLE WHO ACTUALLY COOK

Whether you plan every lunch on Sunday, collect Reels for later, or watch protein and calories without a spreadsheet, Food Planner is the place those habits meet.

Start with a recipe you already want to cook. Build the week from there.
```
