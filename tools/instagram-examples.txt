Hot Honey Garlic Chicken Bread 🥖 🍯 

Ingredients

For the Chicken
1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into small bite-size pieces
1 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp onion powder
1 tsp smoked paprika
1/2 tsp chili powder
1/2 tsp black pepper
Salt to taste
1 tsp cornstarch

For the Hot Honey
1/3 cup honey
2 tbsp hot sauce
1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
1/2 tsp smoked paprika
1 tsp butter
1 tsp lemon juice
Pinch of salt

For the Garlic Butter
4 tbsp salted butter, softened
1 tbsp fresh garlic, finely minced
1 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
1/2 tsp garlic powder
1/4 tsp salt
1 tbsp grated Parmesan

For the Garlic Bread

1 large French or Italian loaf
2 cups shredded mozzarella
1/4 cup grated Parmesan
Fresh parsley, for garnish

For the Hot Honey Garlic Aioli
1/2 cup mayonnaise
2 tbsp hot honey
1 small garlic clove, finely grated
1 tsp lemon juice
1/4 tsp smoked paprika
Pinch of salt

Instructions
Add honey, hot sauce, red pepper flakes, smoked paprika, butter, lemon juice, and salt to a small saucepan. Heat over low heat for 1–2 minutes, stirring until combined and glossy. Set aside.
Add chicken, oil, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, chili powder, black pepper, salt, and cornstarch to a bowl. Toss to coat.
Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add a little oil and cook chicken for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and cooked through.
Mix softened butter, garlic, parsley, garlic powder, salt, and Parmesan until combined.
Mix mayonnaise, hot honey, garlic, lemon juice, smoked paprika, and salt until smooth. Refrigerate until ready to serve.Preheat oven to 375°F. Slice the loaf in half lengthwise and spread generously with garlic butter.
Top with half the mozzarella, hot honey chicken, remaining mozzarella, and Parmesan.
Bake for 10–15 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbly and the edges are golden. Broil for 1–2 minutes if desired, watching closely.
Remove from the oven and drizzle generously with the remaining hot honey. Garnish with fresh parsley and Parmesan.
Slice and serve warm with the hot honey garlic aioli for dipping.

Crispy cheeseburger wraps

Ingredients 

For the beef mixture:

1 lb ground beef ( 80% lean, 20% fat )
2 tbsp oil
1 yellow onion, finely diced 
1 tbsp fresh garlic, minced
1 tbsp tomato paste
Salt to taste
1 tsp black pepper 
1 tbsp garlic powder 
2 tsp smoked paprika powder 
1 tsp onion powder 
1 large jalapeño, chopped
1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce 
1/2 cup water

For the cheese burger sauce:
1/2 cup mayonnaise
2 tbsp ketchup
1 tbsp yellow mustard
1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce 
1 1/2 tbsp finely minced dill pickles
1 tbsp pickle juice
1 tsp finely minced onion
1/2 tsp garlic powder
1/4 tsp smoked paprika
1/2 tsp sugar
1/4 tsp black pepper

To assemble:

9 medium tortilla wraps
1 1/2 cup Mexican style four cheese 
1/2 cup caramelized onions
1/3 cup picked jalapeños, chopped 
Oil for pan frying 

Method:

Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add onion and cook 3–4 minutes until softened. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds.
Add beef and cook, breaking it up, until browned and fully cooked. Drain excess fat if needed, leaving a little behind.
Add tomato paste, salt, pepper, garlic powder, smoked paprika and onion powder. Cook 1–2 minutes. Add Worcestershire sauce jalapeños and water. Simmer uncovered 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the liquid has completely reduced and the beef is moist but not watery. Taste and adjust salt if needed. Remove from heat and cool slightly.

For the sauce, mix mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard, Worcestershire sauce, pickles, pickle juice, onion, garlic powder, smoked paprika, sugar and black pepper until smooth. Refrigerate until ready to use.
Lay out the tortillas and spread about 1 tbsp sauce slightly below the center. Add about 1/4 cup beef, 2 tbsp cheese, caramelized onions and pickled jalapeños. Fold the bottom over the filling, fold in the sides and roll tightly.
Heat a thin layer of oil in a skillet over medium heat. Place wraps seam-side down and cook 2–3 minutes per side, pressing gently, until golden and crispy. Continue cooking until all sides are crisp and the cheese is melted.
Rest 1–2 minutes, slice in half and serve immediately with extra cheeseburger sauce.

Хачапури на завтрак за 15 минут

Такие вкусные мы давно не ели 🔥
Ингредиенты:

300 мл кефира
1 яйцо
1 чл соли
1 чл сахара
3 ст.л раст масла
450 гр муки
10 гр разрыхлителя 

Для начинки:
350 гр сыра (сулугуни)
1 яйцо
Соль по вкусу

Приготовление:
Смешать венчиком все ингредиенты, добавить муку с разрыхлителем и замесить тесто. Натереть сыр, добавить яйцо и перемешать. Тесто и начинку разделить на 4 шарика. Собрать хачапури и обжарить с двух сторон на сухой сковороде. Смазать горячие хачапури сливочным маслом.
Подавать горячими со сладким чаем
